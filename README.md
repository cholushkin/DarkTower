# Dark Tower

**Build your tower. Command your minions. Conquer the world.**

*Dark Tower* is a dark fantasy management and strategy game where you take the role of a Dark Lord building an empire from the shadows.

Recruit and train minions, expand your tower, send raiding parties into the world, and collect treasures to fuel your growing power.

But evil doesn't run itself. Your minions need rest, your prisoners need attention, and payday is always around the corner.

### Features

- 🏰 **Build Your Tower** — Expand and upgrade rooms to strengthen your dark empire.
- 👹 **Manage Your Minions** — Recruit, train, heal, and keep your loyal servants happy (and paid).
- ⚔️ **Raid the World** — Send squads on missions, defeat enemies, and bring home valuable loot.
- 💰 **Collect Treasures** — Manage your inventory and turn stolen goods into gold.
- 🔬 **Research Dark Powers** — Unlock improvements and make your tower more efficient.
- 🌍 **Conquer New Lands** — Progress through increasingly challenging territories.
